# Author review checklist

Review this before marking a chapter manifest approved.

## Canon

- Does every canonical event actually occur in the chapter?
- Are implied facts marked as inference rather than explicit fact?
- Are unnamed characters still unnamed?
- Do source anchors locate the intended passages?
- Has the manifest accidentally corrected or rewritten the prose?

## Adaptation

- Does each scene give the player something meaningful to do, notice, or decide?
- Are all changes from source listed and given an approval state?
- Does the scene order preserve the chapter’s emotional progression?
- Is exposition being converted to environment or interaction where appropriate?
- Are quiet moments allowed to remain quiet?

## Production

- Can environments be assembled from reusable kits?
- Are hero assets truly unique?
- Does every scene list its systems and risks?
- Are sensitive scenes intentionally framed rather than inherited accidentally?
- Can the first version be grayboxed with placeholders?

## Approval states

- `unreviewed` — AI proposal; not authorized.
- `approved_by_author` — deliberate game adaptation.
- `needs_discussion` — potentially useful but unresolved.
- `rejected` — preserve for history; do not implement.
